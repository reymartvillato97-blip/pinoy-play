Estimate your pay, overtime, night differential and deductions.

Estimate only. Enter your actual OT and night-differential rates. Regular pay assumes an 8-hour normal workday; holidays and rest-day work need separate calculations.

13th Month Calculator

Estimate 13th-month pay based on eligible basic salary earned during the calendar year.

Basic formula: eligible basic salary earned ÷ 12. Overtime, night differential, allowances and other payments are generally excluded unless treated as part of basic salary under applicable rules. Actual eligibility and computation may vary.

Calculate shift duration, break time and net hours worked.
